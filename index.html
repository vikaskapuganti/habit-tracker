How it works

Tick a habit when you do it. Missed yesterday? Tap that day in the last-7-days row to fix it.

Current streak counts days in a row up to today. If you haven’t ticked today yet, your streak from yesterday is kept until the day ends.

Your year shows every day as a square. The darker the green, the more habits you completed that day.

Everything is saved on this device only. No login, nothing sent anywhere.
